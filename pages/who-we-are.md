## To climb a mountain

Of course, there are many reasons for this diary. Climbing Cerro Cirripo is one, but combining this with raising funds for a small selection of charities is a major goal. I hope that you can be generous in donating to these charities. Remember I will be taking the pain of climbing through tropical rainforest to cloud forest all the way up to bare rock and freezing temperatures. So give generously, and imagine, rather than experience, the effort.
